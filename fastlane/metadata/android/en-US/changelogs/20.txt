Android Auto, rebuilt on Media3. The car now draws the browse list and player itself: play/pause, +/-30 s on archive tracks, Stop on live, favourite DJs, newest-first sorting, and a Live row inside each DJ folder. Live streams no longer offer seek or skip. Playback and casting stop when you swipe the app away, and your last set resumes when you reconnect to the car. Plus Media3 1.11.1 and Kotlin 2.2 under the hood.
